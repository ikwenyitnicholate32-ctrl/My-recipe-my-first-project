     <h1>chocolate cake recipe</h1>
     <p>Rich, moist, and irresistibly decadent, this chocolate cake is the perfect indulgence for any
occasion. Whether you’re celebrating a special event or simply craving a sweet treat, this recipe
delivers a soft, tender crumb layered with deep chocolate flavor. With simple ingredients and
easy-to-follow steps, you’ll create a bakery-worthy dessert right in your own kitchen. Pair it with a
silky frosting or a dusting of powdered sugar, and you’ll have a crowd-pleasing cake that’s as
beautiful as it is delicious.</p>
   <h2> Ingredients </h2>
   <ul>
     <li>1 ¾ cups flour</li>
     <li>¾ cup cocoa powder</li>
     <li>2 cups sugar</li>
     <li>2 tsp baking powder</li>
     <li>1 ½ tsp baking soda</li>
     <li>1 tsp salt</li>
     <li>2 eggs</li>
     <li>1 cup milk (or buttermilk)</li>
     <li>½ cup vegetable oil</li>
     <li>2 tsp vanilla</li>
     <li>1 cup boiling water (or hot coffee)</li>
     <li>Optional: ½ cup sour cream, 1 cup chocolate chips</li>
    </ul>
    <h2>Instructions</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease two 9-inch pans.</li>
      <li>Mix dry ingredients in one bowl.</li>
      <li>Whisk eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla in another bowl.</li>
      <li>Combine wet and dry, then stir in boiling water/coffee.</li>
      <li>Optional: Fold in sour cream and chocolate chips.</li>
      <li>Pour into pans and bake 30–35 min, until toothpick comes out clean.</li>
      <li>Cool completely before frosting.</li>
      </ol>
